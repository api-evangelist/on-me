# On Me

> On Me (On Me Gifting, Inc., San Francisco) is a digital gifting platform selling wallet-native,
> multi-brand eGift cards organised by hobby and interest rather than by a single retailer. A
> recipient adds the gift to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and taps to pay across any brand in that
> category. Cards are issued by Sutton Bank, Member FDIC. The company emerged from stealth in
> December 2024 with a five-year Mastercard partnership and $1.7M in pre-seed funding led by Lerer
> Hippeau with Focal VC, co-founded by former Google product managers Darragh Meaney (CEO) and
> Sitar Harel.

## API status (measured 2026-08-26)

On Me markets a **Gifting API** at https://onme.com/corporate-gift-cards/gifting-api for
programmatic rewards, incentives, catalog browsing, order creation, delivery by email/SMS/link,
lifecycle status events and webhooks. As of this pass that page is written in the future tense
("Teams **will be able to** issue gifts programmatically") and is the only API surface On Me
publishes. There is:

- no developer portal (https://onme.com/developers -> 404)
- no API reference or docs site (https://onme.com/docs -> 404; no api./docs./developer. subdomain resolves)
- no machine-readable specification (no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL or Postman collection found
  on onme.com, business.onme.com, or the company GitHub org)
- no published base URL, authentication scheme, rate limits, scopes or error catalog
- nothing served under /.well-known/ on either host (all paths 404)

Access is gated behind a sales conversation ("Talk to a specialist / Book a call"). This file
records what is publicly reachable; nothing about the API is inferred or invented.

## Commercial model

- $0 platform fees; no contract and no credit card required to start the business portal
- No published plan tiers — https://onme.com/pricing returns 404
- Revenue comes from merchant commission on redemption, disclosed in the site footer
- Maximum balance on an On Me card is USD 500 (https://onme.com/help/what-is-the-maximum-card-balance)
